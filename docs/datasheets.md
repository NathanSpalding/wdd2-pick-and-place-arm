## Key references

- Al Tahtawi et al. (2021), *Small-scale robot arm design with pick and place mission based on inverse kinematics*, https://doi.org/10.18196/jrc.26124 (the 54 mm benchmark).
- Budynas and Nisbett (2015), *Shigley's Mechanical Engineering Design*, 10th ed. (stress and bearing life).
- Craig (2005), *Introduction to Robotics: Mechanics and Control* (kinematics).
- ISO 9283:1998, *Manipulating industrial robots: performance criteria and related test methods* (accuracy and repeatability).
- Carpentier et al. (2019), Pinocchio, and Caron, Pink (inverse kinematics libraries used by the software).
